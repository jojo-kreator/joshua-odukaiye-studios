Studio portraits and live moments, photographed with the same care.

Joshua Odukaiye Studios is a photography studio in Lagos, Nigeria. The work moves between two rooms: the controlled light of the portrait studio, and the unrepeatable moments of worship gatherings and live recording sessions.

In the studio, each portrait is lit to suit the person in front of the lens, whether it is a headshot, a graduation session or a fashion and editorial concept. At weddings, worship gatherings and recording sessions, the aim is to record the room as it felt, with quiet attention to faces, instruments and atmosphere.

Based in Lagos and available to travel across Nigeria and internationally. Finished photographs are delivered in a private online gallery.

  • Studio portraits and headshots
  • Graduation sessions
  • Fashion and editorial
  • Weddings and events
  • Worship gatherings
  • Live recording sessions
